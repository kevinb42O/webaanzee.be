GENERATIE

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE extraordinary high-end modern horizontal brand signature for "webaanzee", a boutique Belgian coastal web design studio.

Art direction: quiet architectural luxury meets contemporary independent design culture. A real distinctive brand identity with an ORIGINAL SCULPTURAL EMBLEM plus a bespoke wordmark, conceived as ONE balanced lockup. Sophisticated, restrained, intelligent, refined. Avoid a logo that is simply a name typed in a font. Avoid a gaming or esports aesthetic.

Emblem: a compact abstract continuous folded ribbon that brings together a W-like tidal rhythm and a precise horizontal coastline in a single sculptural form. Two elegantly interlocked angular loops, just enough curvature to suggest water, crisp deliberate cuts, beautifully resolved negative space. Build an ownable geometric symbol, not a literal wave illustration, not a plain W letter, not code brackets, not an infinity sign. Flat burnished terracotta/coral #d86548, with a SMALL amount of deep tonal modeling to make the folded structure legible. No shiny chrome, no glass, no excessive 3D. It should feel like a perfectly designed small bronze architectural object reduced to a graphic symbol.

Wordmark: exact text "webaanzee", all lowercase, complete spelling w e b a a n z e e. Bespoke contemporary sophisticated sans lettering: medium weight, subtly squared circular bowls, precise open counters, straight disciplined stems, very refined optical kerning. A subtle unique joining detail between the final "zee" gives it a custom signature. Elegant and grounded, not ultra-bold, not cartoon-round, not italic, not a generic font. Pure soft ivory #f5f2eb. The symbol must be optically compact, approximately 1.25 times the lowercase wordmark x-height, and no taller than the b ascender. Align symbol and lettering carefully on their optical center. A restrained gap approximately the width of the e counter. The full name follows the emblem; never remove the w from the name.

Composition: the complete horizontal lockup centered, one emblem to left and wordmark to right, cohesive restrained silhouette, approximately 6:1 overall ink aspect ratio. Only one logo. Minimal safe margins around ink. Entire background perfectly flat pure black #000000 for clean production extraction. Large high-resolution raster with crisp vector-like edges.
No slogan, no descriptors, no website address, no extra text, no dots, no border, no underline, no generic ocean sunset, no literal boat or lighthouse, no decorative lines, no gradients on letters, no shadows cast behind logo, no texture, no glow, no presentation mockup, no alternatives, no fine decorative detail that fails at navbar size.

VERFIJNING

Refine this identity into a finished premium horizontal logo. Preserve the exact spelling webaanzee and preserve the crafted geometric wordmark with its distinctive joined zee detail. Preserve the original sculptural coral folded-ribbon coastal emblem, its exact geometry, colors and calm tonal folds. Make only a compositional refinement: reduce the emblem uniformly to approximately 60 percent of its current size, so it is a compact signature about the height of the wordmark, and set it close to the left of the name with a deliberate clean gap equal to half a letter e width. Keep the entire emblem visible. Align both elements on their optical center. The name should occupy roughly 80 percent of the total logo width. Tighten the overall canvas to the complete horizontal logo with only 3 percent safe padding. Keep warm ivory lettering, coral symbol, and a completely pure flat black #000000 background. Eliminate surface mottling and paper texture from both symbol and letters: smooth solid lettering, smooth controlled sculptural symbol. No additional lettering, no dots or registration marks, no tagline, no underline, no extra shape, no cast shadow. High-end editorial brand signature, clear at navbar size.
